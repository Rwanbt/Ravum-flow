# S9 — confirmatory campaign (H4 protocol v1.0), 2026-11-15

**Result: the H4 success criterion was NOT met. On this workload Flow (arm G) is slower than the
instrumented O0 baseline (arm E), not faster.** The raw record is
[`out/campaign-h4.json`](out/campaign-h4.json); the frozen protocol is
[`docs/BENCHMARK_S9.md`](../../docs/BENCHMARK_S9.md) § Protocol v1.0.

## What ran

| Field | Value |

## Result

| Arm | n | median `T_verified` | min | max | mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G (Flow)** | 10 | **4.925 s** | 4.655 s | 12.058 s | 5.746 s |
| **E (O0)** | 10 | **2.823 s** | 2.579 s | 3.503 s | 2.891 s |

Paired: median difference **+2.153 s**, median ratio **1.784**, G slower in **10/10** pairs,
one-sided sign test **p = 0.000977**.

**H4 criterion: paired ratio ≤ 0.70 with p < 0.05. Observed ratio 1.784 (G ~78 % slower). Failed.**

|---|---|---|---|

## Interpretation (what this does and does not show)

- The gap is dominated by G's integration phase: the candidate `run` materialises the exact tree in
  an isolated scratch directory and runs the required obligation there with a **cold** Cargo target
  directory. E runs each agent's test in a warm per-agent target directory and a warm final test.
  That is a real design cost of isolated re-verification, not a measurement artifact — but it means
  this workload measures *isolation overhead* more than coordination benefit.
- The workload is single-host, single-writer-per-module and conflict-free: none of Flow's claimed
  value (attribution, exact-tree receipts, collision refusal, publication CAS) is exercised. A
  conflict-heavy workload is the case where E's merge step would cost more; it was **not** measured
  here.
- Agent thinking time is a constant (0 ms); LLM latency and human supervision are out of scope.

## Verdict and consequences

- **No productivity advantage is claimed.** `docs/CAPABILITIES.md` keeps productivity
  `SPECIFIED_ONLY` / `UNPROVEN`; the README makes no speed claim.
- `D3` is **not closed as a positive result**: the campaign ran as pre-registered, and it produced a
  negative result on the frozen workload. A future campaign must be a **new protocol version** with
  its own pre-registration (for example a conflict-heavy workload, or warm-cache-equalized arms).
- The raw data is kept: a negative result is evidence, not a failure to report.

## Reproduce

```bash
python experiments/s9/run_s9.py --arms GE --runs 10 --confirmatory --out campaign-h4.json
```

The harness writes `kind: CONFIRMATORY`, the frozen H4 fields, every per-run record and the paired
statistics. It refuses `--confirmatory` with fewer than 10 runs or arms other than `GE`.
